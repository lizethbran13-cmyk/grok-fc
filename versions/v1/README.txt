GROK FC
=======

Arcade 7v7 football with slide tackles and a strict referee.
Bright pitch, Grok orange/cyan kits vs Rival magenta.

Run: cd grok-fc && python3 -m http.server 8080
Open http://localhost:8080

Controls: WASD move, J/X pass, K/C shoot, L/V/Space slide, Tab switch, Esc pause.
Mobile: stick + PASS/SHOOT/SLIDE/SWAP.

Slide tackles: timing matters. Late/from behind = foul.
Ref is strict: free kicks, pens, yellows, reds, advantage, send-offs.
Cards fire often when play is physical. Away AI slides aggressively.

7v7 roles GK/DEF/MID/FWD. 2 halves. Goals, HT, FT, rematch.
Files: index.html style.css game.js README.txt
