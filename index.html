HOW TO PLAY

Score more than them!

📱 Phone

  • Left thumb anywhere = joystick
  • PASS — passes to the teammate you point at (dashed ring)
  • SHOOT — hold for power, let go to shoot. Aims for the open corner.
  • SPRINT — hold while you have the ball
  • Defending: TACKLE near the ball, PRESS (hold) to chase, SWITCH to change player

⌨️ Keyboard

  • WASD/Arrows move · Shift sprint
  • J/Z pass (switch when defending)
  • K/X shoot — hold for power (press when defending)
  • L/C/Space tackle · Q/Tab switch · Esc pause

⭐ You always control the player with the yellow arrow. It switches automatically to whoever is closest to the ball. Tackle from the front — tackles from behind or after the ball has gone are fouls, and really reckless ones get a card.
