HOW TO PLAY

Score more than them!

📱 Phone

  • Left thumb anywhere on the left = joystick. Up on the stick = up the pitch.
  • PASS — to the teammate you point at (white ring shows who)
  • SHOOT — hold for power, let go. It aims for the open corner for you.
  • SPRINT — hold to run faster
  • Defending: big button = TACKLE, SWITCH = change player, hold SPRINT (no stick) to chase the ball

⌨️ Keyboard

  • WASD/Arrows move · Shift/L sprint
  • J/Z pass (switch player when defending)
  • K/X/Space shoot — hold for power (tackle when defending)
  • Q/Tab switch · Esc pause

⭐ You always control the player with the yellow arrow. It switches automatically to whoever is closest to the ball, and the ball sticks to your feet when you dribble. Tackle from the front — tackles from behind or after the ball has gone are fouls, and really reckless ones get a card.
